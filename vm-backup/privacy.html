Privacy policy

Duke AQ VM Backup Retention
Updated November 12, 2026

Purpose and Google access

This single-user utility preserves backup-transfer records from the owner’s Windows virtual machine in the owner’s Google Drive. It requests Drive permission to identify the authorized account and upload, list, and download archives. Google’s permission is broader than one folder; the utility’s configuration restricts its operations to the designated archive folder.

Processing and storage

Backup records and archive metadata are processed on the owner’s VM and stored in the owner’s Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in a protected VM configuration. Integrity-check reports remain under the owner’s control.

Sharing and use

The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share backup contents with other users. It does not publish backups on this website. Google processes data under its own service policies.

Retention and control

Cloud archives are retained. Eligible local backup-transfer records are removed only after successful cloud-download and content-integrity verification, retaining at least the most recent seven days locally. The owner can stop the scheduled task, revoke access in Google Account settings, and manage archives directly in Drive.

This website

These static information pages contain no backup browser, login form, app-added analytics, or access to backup contents. GitHub Pages may process visitor information under GitHub’s policies.
